How It Works

Onboarding in
4 simple steps

1

Send Your First Message

Message the MedAkcess WhatsApp number. Your profile is created automatically from your phone number — no forms, no sign-up.

2

Give Consent

You'll receive a welcome message with a clear explanation of how your data is used. Reply "AGREE" to proceed. Your consent is recorded with a timestamp.

3

Select a Doctor

Browse a numbered list of verified doctors with their specialisation and years of experience. Reply with a number to choose your doctor.

4

You're Connected

A conversation is created and your doctor is notified in real-time. You can now send your medical question — text, images, documents, or voice notes.
